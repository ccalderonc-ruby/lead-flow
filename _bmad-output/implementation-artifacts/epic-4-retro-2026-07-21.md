## Challenges

- Change volume hard for the Project Lead to track while agents ship quickly.
- Unclear when to fix bugs vs defer polish vs wait until end-of-course — fear of end-loaded breakage.
- Epic 4 sat as a large uncommitted stack until merge; overlapping kickoff PR (#24) added noise.
- Modal/drawer a11y (Escape / focus trap / restore) was parked repeatedly until promoted during this retro.

## Key insights

1. **Visibility beats velocity** — merge to `dev` and labeled findings matter as much as shipping features.
2. **Explicit triage stops “later” black holes** — patch now / before next epic / park-with-label (`docs/BUG_POLISH_TRIAGE.md`).
3. **Promote parked debt when it hurts comprehension or demos** — a11y was fixed in-retro once prioritized (`useDialogA11y`).
4. **Kickoff prep works** — shared kit before Epic 4 forms prevented a third modal dialect.
